Cooperative Localization Pipeline HETEROGENEOUS · SENSOR-AGNOSTIC · COMMUNICATION-AGNOSTIC · ADAPTIVE V2X

From heterogeneous perception to robust and precise urban positioning. LiDAR, camera and multimodal fused 3D bounding boxes from the ego vehicle, a partner vehicle and roadside infrastructure are associated into shared dynamic landmarks (cars, cyclists, pedestrians) and fused in a stacked-measurement EKF with a data-driven cooperative covariance and automatic V2X / V2I / V2V / GNSS fallback. Evaluated on the UrbanIng-V2X dataset against EKF GNSS/IMU, FAST-LIO2, V2I-Pose and V2I-Map (NDT).
